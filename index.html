Day-by-Day Itinerary

Upon arrival at Bandaranaike International Airport, meet our representative and transfer to Sigiriya. Check in at Kassapa Lion Rock Hotel and enjoy a relaxing day after your flight.

🤝 Airport Pick-Up 🚗 Transfer to Sigiriya
Overnight: Sigiriya — Kassapa Lion Rock (Half Board)

After breakfast, enjoy a traditional village experience in Hiriwadunna, including a Land master ride, a village walk and a traditional Sri Lankan lunch.

In the afternoon, visit Polonnaruwa Ancient City, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, and explore its historical ruins, ancient temples and monuments.

🚜 Hiriwadunna Village Experience 🍛 Traditional Sri Lankan Lunch 🏛️ Polonnaruwa Ancient City
Overnight: Sigiriya — Kassapa Lion Rock (Half Board)

Early morning visit to the famous Sigiriya Lion Rock Fortress, a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Enjoy the spectacular views and explore this remarkable ancient kingdom.

Return to the hotel and relax. In the afternoon, enjoy a traditional Ayurvedic massage.

🥾 Sigiriya Lion Rock Fortress 💆 Traditional Ayurvedic Massage
Overnight: Sigiriya — Kassapa Lion Rock (Half Board)

After breakfast, visit the Dambulla Royal Cave Temple, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, famous for its ancient Buddhist paintings and impressive Buddha statues.

In the afternoon, enjoy a traditional Sri Lankan cultural dance performance.

🛕 Dambulla Royal Cave Temple 💃 Cultural Dance Show
Overnight: Sigiriya — Kassapa Lion Rock (Half Board)

After breakfast, check out and travel towards Kandy. En route, visit a traditional Spice Garden in Matale and learn about Sri Lanka's famous spices and Ayurvedic herbs.

In the afternoon, visit the Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic, one of Sri Lanka's most important Buddhist religious sites. Enjoy a relaxing walk around Kandy Lake.

🌶️ Matale Spice Garden 🛕 Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic 🚶 Kandy Lake Walk
Overnight: Kandy — Thilanka Hotel (Half Board)

After breakfast, travel towards Nuwara Eliya through beautiful mountain scenery.

Visit a tea plantation and tea factory to discover how world-famous Ceylon Tea is produced. Continue to Ramboda Falls to enjoy the beautiful waterfall and surrounding scenery.

☕ Tea Plantation & Factory 🌊 Ramboda Falls
Overnight: Nuwara Eliya — Araliya Red (Half Board)

After breakfast, transfer to Nanu Oya Railway Station and enjoy one of the world's most beautiful scenic train journeys to Ella, passing through lush green tea plantations, mountains and picturesque villages.

Upon arrival, transfer to Ella Flower Garden Hotel and relax.

🚂 Scenic Train Ride to Ella

Please note: Train tickets are subject to availability.

Overnight: Ella — Ella Flower Garden (Bed & Breakfast)

After breakfast, explore the famous Nine Arch Bridge, one of the most photographed attractions in Sri Lanka.

Continue to Little Adam's Peak for a beautiful nature walk and breathtaking panoramic views of the Ella mountains. Enjoy the relaxed atmosphere of Ella.

🌉 Nine Arch Bridge 🥾 Little Adam's Peak
Overnight: Ella — Ella Flower Garden (Bed & Breakfast)

After breakfast, check out and travel towards Yala. En route, visit the beautiful Ravana Falls.

In the afternoon, enjoy an exciting wildlife safari in Yala National Park, where you may have the opportunity to see elephants, crocodiles, deer, bears and the famous Sri Lankan leopard.

🌊 Ravana Falls 🐆 Yala National Park Safari
Overnight: Yala — Tamarind Tree Hotel (Half Board)

After breakfast, travel towards Hikkaduwa on the southern coast of Sri Lanka.

Check in at Hikka Tranz by Cinnamon and enjoy the swimming pool, beach and relaxing tropical atmosphere.

🚗 Drive to Hikkaduwa 🏖️ Beach & Pool Relaxation
Overnight: Hikkaduwa — Hikka Tranz by Cinnamon (Half Board)

After breakfast, enjoy a scenic boat ride along the Madu River, exploring its beautiful mangroves and small islands.

Visit a sea turtle hatchery and learn about local turtle conservation efforts.

In the evening, explore the historic Galle Fort, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, with its charming streets, colonial architecture, cafés and beautiful ocean views. Return to the hotel in Hikkaduwa.

🚤 Madu River Boat Safari 🐢 Sea Turtle Hatchery 🏰 Galle Fort
Overnight: Hikkaduwa — Hikka Tranz by Cinnamon (Half Board)

After breakfast, check out from the hotel and transfer to Bandaranaike International Airport for your departure flight.

End of a wonderful Sri Lankan holiday!

✈️ Airport Transfer
Departure Transfer: Transfer to Bandaranaike International Airport after breakfast.
